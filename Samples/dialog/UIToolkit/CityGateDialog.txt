// City Gate sample authored in Yarn-style plain text (Unity TextAsset).
// Assign this file to DialogRunnerBehaviour._initialScript or a SampleBootstrap._dialogScript.

<<declare $gold = 60>>
<<declare $met_guard = false>>
<<declare $gate_open = false>>

title: gate_arrival
---
Narrator: Rain drums against the iron portcullis. You count {$gold} gold in your pouch.
<<jump guard_greeting>>
===

title: guard_greeting
---
<<set $met_guard = true>>
Gate Guard: Halt, traveler. The North Gate is sealed tonight. What business brings you here? (Gold: {$gold})
-> Offer 50 gold to open the gate <<if $gold >= 50 and !$gate_open>>
    <<set $gold = $gold - 50>>
    <<play_sfx coins_clink>>
    <<jump guard_bribed>>
-> Ask if there is any work (+20 gold)
    <<jump guard_tip>>
-> Step through the open gate <<if $gate_open>>
    <<jump enter_city>>
-> Turn back into the rain
    <<jump walk_away>>
===

title: guard_tip
---
<<set $gold += 20>>
<<grant_bonus 20>>
Gate Guard: Drop this lost coin purse at the watchpost? Actually, keep the 20 gold finder's fee. Now you have {$gold} gold.
<<jump guard_greeting>>
===

title: guard_bribed
---
<<set $gate_open = true>>
<<open_gate north_gate>>
Gate Guard: Fifty gold... fair enough. Step lively before the captain spots us. You have {$gold} gold left.
<<jump guard_greeting>>
===

title: enter_city
---
Narrator: The heavy iron gate groans open. Warm lantern light spills onto the cobblestones ahead.
===

title: walk_away
---
Gate Guard: Wise choice. Come back at dawn.
===
